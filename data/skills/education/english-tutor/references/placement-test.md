# Placement test

Goal: estimate CEFR level per skill (grammar/vocabulary, reading, writing) in 15-20 minutes. Listening and speaking stay "unassessed" until audio is enabled.

## Part 1: grammar and vocabulary ladder
- Ask 4 items per level, from A2 up to C1, one at a time (gap-fill or choose the correct form).
- Stop climbing after 2 misses at a level in the same part. Level = highest level with 3 of 4 correct.
- Include Spanish-speaker traps: false friends, prepositions, articles, make/do, present perfect vs simple past.

## Part 2: reading
- One passage of about 150 words at B2, then 3 questions (main idea, detail, inference).
- If 3/3, give a C1 passage (about 220 words, academic tone) with 3 questions.

## Part 3: writing
- Task: 100-130 words, opinion on a simple topic ("Should students wear uniforms?").
- Score 1-5 on: task response, organization, grammar range and accuracy, vocabulary range. Map the average to a CEFR band.

## Output
- Write per-skill estimates and a confidence note in `Tutor/Profile.md`.
- Write the first weekly focus in `Tutor/Plan.md`.
- Log the session in `Tutor/Log.md`.
- Tell the learner the result in 5 lines or fewer: strengths, main gaps, what the first week will focus on.
